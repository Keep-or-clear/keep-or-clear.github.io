Privacy Policy

Keep or Clear for Android

Photo access and reminders

Keep or Clear uses Android’s media library to find photos on your device, build previews, and suggest photos for review. On Android 14 and later, you can grant access to selected photos. Full access lets the app select from your library for manual review and recurring reminders.

Notifications are optional. If you enable reminders, Android schedules them on your device and can resume scheduling after a restart. Whether previews appear on your lock screen depends on your Android notification settings.

Collection and sharing

Keep or Clear processes photos on your device. It has no account, ads, analytics, or crash reporting service, and it does not send photos, review history, or usage data to a developer server.

If you choose Share or Open in gallery, Android gives the app you select temporary read access to the current photo. That app handles the photo under its own privacy policy.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Settings, review decisions, photo references, and trash and storage records stay in app-private storage. They support review, reminders, undo, and storage totals. Android backup is disabled for this data. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes these records from the device.

Android asks for confirmation before the app moves a photo to trash or restores one. Android controls trash expiry. To permanently remove photos through the app, you choose which items to empty and confirm deletion. Clearing the app’s storage does not remove the original photos.
